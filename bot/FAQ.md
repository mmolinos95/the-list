## How the list works

- Each practice has one or more lists (like Skills and Games), each with a cap. When a list is full, you go on its waitlist.
- The waitlist moves up on its own when someone drops out, until the list locks.
- Lists lock 2 hours before the first list of the practice starts. After that, nobody can sign up and nobody moves up on their own; the board can still move someone up.
- Dropping out after the lock is a late drop.
